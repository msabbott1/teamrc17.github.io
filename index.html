The TeamRC17 mail list is now hosted by Google Groups. If you have stopped receiving mails from the list please send a mail to teamrc17+subscribe(at)googlegroups.com. To post to the list use teamrc17(at)googlegroups.com.

Welcome to the largest on-line resource site for Honda’s RC17 (also known as the CBX750E/F/G) motorcycle.

We also have some information available for the RC18 (CBX750P), RC20 (NightHawk 700), and RC42 (CB750) variants.

If you’re a new visitor, check out the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) first, then have a ride through the site.

If you’re a regular visitor, check the News section to see what’s been updated in the last three months.

2020 is our 23rd year in operation, and we’d like to thank all the past and present members for their support over the past years. Cheers!
