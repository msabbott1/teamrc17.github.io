The Team RC17 group is open to anyone with an interest in the RC17 (CBX750). The list currently has around 150 members based in all continents. The primary aim of the list is to give support to RC17 owners. If you have any questions then there is a high chance that someone here will help you.

If you would like to join the team please send a mail to teamrc17+subscribe(at)googlegroups.com from the email address that you want to subscribe . To post to the list use teamrc17(at)googlegroups.com.ink below.

To unsubscribe send mail to teamrc17+unsubscribe(at)googlegroups.com

When you join the mailing list, you automatically become an unallocated Team member.
